# Foundation

## Purpose

Project 0 preserves enough shared structure for meaning to survive movement between people, agents, applications, and time.

The target is **collective continuity among sovereign participants**: cooperation without forced sameness, shared memory without a single unquestionable narrator, and interoperability without centralizing every product.

## Design pressures

### Participation × repetition, coupled consistently

Participation creates contact. Repetition creates return. Consistency is the quality of their coupling. Together they can produce continuity; separately they tend toward souvenir, habit, or predictability.

### Relationship enables decompression

An isolated artifact is radically underdetermined. Meaning becomes recoverable when its relationships are available: who observed it, what preceded it, what it answers, what contradicted it, under which authority it moved, and how later use transformed it.

### Continuity is not consensus

The system preserves disagreement, uncertainty, rejected parallels, and abandoned proposals. It does not flatten them into one answer.

### Sovereignty is composable

Participation does not imply unlimited disclosure, transfer, interpretation, or control. Authority must remain explicit, scoped, attributable, and exhaustible.

## What Project 0 is

- a semantic interoperability contract
- a lineage and receipt model
- a vocabulary for bounded authority
- a home for canonical fixtures and conformance tests
- a stable interface between otherwise independent projects

## What Project 0 is not

- the user-facing Full Measure world
- TranchNode itself
- a replacement for application databases
- an omniscient archive
- an identity provider
- a model prompt collection
- a license for agents to rewrite human memory

## Order of precedence

1. Human dignity and stated boundaries
2. Provenance and disclosure constraints
3. Project 0 invariants
4. Domain-specific contracts
5. Product convenience
6. Model confidence or implementation accident
